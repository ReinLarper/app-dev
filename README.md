
# **Limbus Company**
---

## *Short Summary*

> "This is the story of a manager, twelve Sinners, and a journey through the worst possible city in the world."

You are **Dante**. Not the other Dante from *Devil May Cry*, nor the other Dante from *Fate*. You are the manager of **Limbus Company**, tasked with handling twelve questionable individuals known as the *Sinners*.

Your mission? Traverse the City, face its horrors, and retrieve the mysterious **Golden Boughs**.

Sounds simple, right?

**WRONG.**

Expect:
- Character development.
- Psychological trauma.
- Questionable life decisions.
- Philosophical discussions.
- Absolutely insane boss fights.
- Some of the most memorable OSTs.

And of course...

***Playing Leviathan OST***

## ⭐ Why I Like Limbus Company

1. **Storytelling** – A fascinating world filled with mysteries and lore.
2. **Characters** – Each Sinner has their own personality, struggles, and development.
3. **Music** – The soundtrack goes incredibly hard. MORE MILI SONGS AND STUDIO EIM
4. **Worldbuilding** – The City is a terrifying yet fascinating place.
5. **Emotional Damage** – You will laugh, suffer, and probably question your life choices.

## ⚠️ A Small Warning

**Be prepared for:**
- Heavy story themes.
- Psychological horror.
- Violence and disturbing imagery.
- Complicated lore.
- Emotional moments that hit harder than expected.

---

## Final Thoughts

**Limbus Company** is more than just a game about managing twelve Sinners. It is a journey through a bizarre world filled with suffering, ambition, identity, and humanity.

And remember:

*You are Dante.*

*You have twelve Sinners.*

*You have a mission.*
